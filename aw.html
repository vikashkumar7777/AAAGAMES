Alan Wake 2

About the Game

Alan Wake 2 is a psychological survival horror game developed by Remedy Entertainment. The story follows writer Alan Wake and FBI agent Saga Anderson as they become involved in a supernatural mystery surrounding the town of Bright Falls.

Players explore two interconnected realities while solving mysteries, investigating crime scenes and fighting terrifying supernatural enemies. The game combines cinematic storytelling, exploration and intense survival-horror gameplay.

Genre: Survival Horror, Psychological Horror, Thriller

Developer: Remedy Entertainment

Release Date: October 27, 2023
