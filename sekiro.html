About the Game

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice is an action-adventure game set in late 1500s Sengoku Japan. Play as the "One-Armed Wolf", a shinobi tasked with protecting his young master while battling powerful enemies and uncovering the secrets of immortality.

Genre: Action-Adventure

Developer: FromSoftware
