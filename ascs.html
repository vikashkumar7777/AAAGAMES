About the Game

Assassin's Creed Shadows is an open-world action RPG set in 16th-century Japan during the late Sengoku period. Play as two very different protagonists, the shinobi Naoe and the legendary samurai Yasuke, as they navigate a world filled with conflict, political intrigue, and the struggle between light and shadow.

Genre: Action RPG, Open World, Stealth

Developer: Ubisoft Quebec

Release Date: 2025
