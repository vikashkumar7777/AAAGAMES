About the Game

Grand Theft Auto V is an open-world action-adventure game set in Los Santos and Blaine County. Follow the stories of Michael, Franklin, and Trevor as their lives become intertwined through a series of dangerous heists and criminal adventures.

Genre: Action, Adventure, Open World
