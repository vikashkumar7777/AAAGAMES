About the Game

Red Dead Redemption 2 is an open-world action-adventure game set in the American frontier during the late 1800s. Follow Arthur Morgan and the Van der Linde gang as they struggle to survive while the era of outlaws and gunslingers begins to disappear.

Genre: Action-Adventure, Open World

Developer: Rockstar Games

Release Date: 2018
