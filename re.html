About the Game

Resident Evil 4 is a survival horror action game and a remake of the legendary 2005 classic. Play as Leon S. Kennedy, who is sent to a remote European village to rescue the President's daughter. Facing terrifying enemies, mysterious cultists, and horrifying creatures, Leon must fight his way through a nightmare filled with danger and secrets.

Genre: Survival Horror, Action-Adventure

Developer: Capcom

Release Date: 2023
