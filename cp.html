About the Game

Cyberpunk 2077 is an open-world action RPG set in Night City, a futuristic metropolis obsessed with power, glamour, and body modification. Play as V, a mercenary searching for a one-of-a-kind implant that could hold the key to immortality while navigating a dangerous world of corporations, gangs, and cybernetic technology.

Genre: Action RPG, Open World

Developer: CD PROJEKT RED

Release Date: 2020
