About the Game

God of War is an action-adventure game that follows Kratos and his son Atreus as they journey through the Norse realms. After the death of Kratos' wife, they set out to scatter her ashes from the highest peak in all the realms, facing dangerous creatures, powerful gods and the challenges of their journey.

Genre: Action-Adventure

Developer: Santa Monica Studio

Release Date: 2018
