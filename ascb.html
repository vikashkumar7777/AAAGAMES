About the Game

Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced is a ground-up remake of the iconic pirate adventure. Sail across the Caribbean as Edward Kenway during the Golden Age of Piracy, exploring a vast open world while battling enemies on land and at sea. The remake features enhanced visuals, improved gameplay, new content, and a modernized Anvil Engine.

Genre: Action-Adventure, Open World

Developer: Ubisoft Singapore

Release Date: July 9, 2026
