WWE 2K26

About the Game

WWE 2K26 is a professional wrestling simulation game developed by Visual Concepts and published by 2K. The game features more than 400 playable Superstars and Legends, making it the largest roster in franchise history.

WWE 2K26 introduces new match types including I Quit, Inferno, Three Stages of Hell and Dumpster matches, along with improved gameplay, interactive environments and new weapons.

Genre: Sports, Wrestling, Simulation

Developer: Visual Concepts

Release Date: March 12, 2026
