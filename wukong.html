About the Game

Black Myth: Wukong is an action RPG game inspired by Chinese Mythology and "Journey to the West". You shall set out as the Destined One to venture into the challenges and marvels ahead, to uncover the obscured truth beneath the veil of a glorious legend from the past.

Genre: Action RPG
