About the Game

Ghost of Tsushima is an open-world action-adventure game set in 13th-century Japan. Play as Jin Sakai, a samurai fighting to protect the island of Tsushima from the Mongol invasion while forging a new path as the Ghost.

Genre: Action-Adventure, Open World

Developer: Sucker Punch Productions

Release Date: 2020
