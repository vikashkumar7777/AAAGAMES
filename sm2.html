About the Game

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 continues the adventures of Peter Parker and Miles Morales as they protect New York City from powerful new threats. With two playable Spider-Men, players can switch between Peter and Miles while using new abilities, suits, and web-swinging mechanics to take on villains including Kraven the Hunter and Venom.

Genre: Action-Adventure, Open World

Developer: Insomniac Games, Nixxes Software

Release Date: 2023
