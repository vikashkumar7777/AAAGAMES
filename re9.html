About the Game

Resident Evil Requiem is the ninth mainline entry in the Resident Evil series. The game follows FBI intelligence analyst Grace Ashcroft as she investigates a mysterious death at an abandoned hotel, while veteran agent Leon S. Kennedy investigates a new series of deaths connected to the horrors of the past.

Genre: Survival Horror, Action-Adventure

Developer: Capcom

Release Date: 2026
